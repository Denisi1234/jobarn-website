For English clients: Jobarn Now Building Custom Software Tailored for Your Business — we expand our Software Development & Licensing division to deliver ERP, POS & MIS built around your exact workflow, fully integrated and licensed.

Hospital Management Systems (registration, billing, lab, pharmacy), ERP for trading/manufacturing, POS for retail, inventory & procurement, HR & payroll, school/church management. Kila app inaunganishwa — tunachanganya legacy zako na mpya ili data isambae.

  • Custom Development: Built from scratch after deep workflow discovery — not off-the-shelf.
  • System Integration: Tunachanganya legacy na software mpya ili taarifa zisambae idara kwa idara.
  • Licensing & Compliance: Microsoft, Adobe, Autodesk — 100% compliant.
  • Maintenance & Updates: Patches, security updates, optimization — huna haja ya IT ya ndani.
