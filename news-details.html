Jobarn Sasa Tupo SkyCity Mall, Floor ya Kwanza — Na Huduma Mpya ya Custom Software

SkyCity Mall, Dar es Salaam 25th September 2025 by Jobarn Team
▶ Tazama Video ya Ofisi Yetu Mpya — Real Video

Habari njema kwa wateja wote wa Jobarn General Trading Company Ltd! Kuanzia sasa, tunapatikana rasmi SkyCity Mall, Floor ya Kwanza, Off Mlimani City, University Road, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Kuhama kwetu si tu mabadiliko ya anwani — ni hatua ya kimkakati ya kukusogezea huduma zetu karibu zaidi, kwa mazingira ya kisasa, salama na yanayofikika kwa urahisi kutoka maeneo yote ya jiji.

For our English-speaking clients: Jobarn has officially moved to SkyCity Mall, 1st Floor, off Mlimani City, University Road, Dar es Salaam. Whether you need enterprise laptops, servers, CCTV or a fully custom ERP, our new showroom and support center is now open Monday–Friday 8am–5pm and Saturday by appointment. Call 0716026781 / 0745912000 or email info@jobarn.co.tz / contact@jobarn.co.tz for directions.

1. Kwa Nini SkyCity Mall? — Why We Moved

Uamuzi wa kuhamia SkyCity Mall umetokana na maoni yenu. Mlikuwa mnalalamikia umbali, foleni na kukosa sehemu ya kuegesha. Sasa, SkyCity inatupa:

  • Accessibility: Ipo katikati ya jiji, pembezoni mwa Mlimani City, na daladala/bajaji nyingi zinaishia hapo.
  • Parking & Security: Maegesho ya kutosha, ulinzi wa masaa 24 na CCTV ya kisasa.
  • Showroom Kubwa: Unaweza kuona na kugusa vifaa halisi — laptop, server, switch, router na accessories kabla ya kununua.
  • One-Stop Support: Mauzo, installation, licensing na help desk vyote sehemu moja — Consultation → Planning → Implementation → Support.

We designed the 1st Floor space around our 4-stage methodology. Unapoingia, timu yetu inakufanyia Consultation ya bure kuchambua changamoto zako, kisha Planning ya blueprint, Implementation bila kusimamisha kazi zako, na Support ya muda mrefu.

“Tumekuja karibu zaidi ili kukuhudumia haraka zaidi — SkyCity Mall ni nyumbani mpya ya Jobarn, na mlango wetu uko wazi kwa kila mfanyabiashara.” — Jobarn Management

2. Video Tour — Tazama Ofisi Yetu Mpya

Kwenye video hapo juu, utaona mapokezi, chumba cha mikutano, showroom ya vifaa na technical bench ambapo mafundi wetu hufanya diagnostics. Ikiwa huwezi kufika leo, bofya play uone mazingira halisi. Pia tunapanga live session wiki ijayo kujibu maswali yako kuhusu kuhama kwetu.
