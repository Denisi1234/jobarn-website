Jobarn Project Details — Enterprise ICT Deployments

Corporate Campus Network, CCTV & ERP Deployment

Jobarn designed and delivered a complete ICT overhaul for a corporate client in Dar es Salaam: forty high-end workstations, a ventilated server room with backup power, structured Cat6A and fiber backbone across three floors, forty HD CCTV cameras with central monitoring, and a custom ERP with POS integration — completed in six weeks with zero business downtime.

All hardware was sourced from global brands with official warranty, installed by certified Jobarn engineers and handed over with documentation, staff training and a 24/7 support agreement. Network speed increased tenfold, incident response is now instant, and management runs purchasing, stock and billing from one dashboard.

Project Results & Benefits

Following our Consultation, Planning, Implementation and Support framework, the client received measurable results within the first month of go-live:

  • 10x faster office network with 99% uptime since handover
  • Full HD CCTV coverage with real-time monitoring and playback
  • Custom ERP and POS cutting billing and stock-taking time by half
